# Rules for the assistant

Follow the creator's current request and established approval scope. These boundaries protect originals and truthful output; they are not a reason to repeat a decision the creator already made.

1. **Source and speech.** Work only with files the creator names and their assigned roles. Preserve originals and recorded meaning, order and speed unless a specific speech/cut change is authorized. Literal subtitles use reviewed recorded words. Correct clear ASR errors against the recording; flag uncertain words instead of pretending the draft is verified. Do not synthesize or replace dialogue without explicit authorization.
2. **Explanatory text.** Reviewed titles, labels and diagrams may summarize or explain the idea without pretending to be verbatim subtitles. Keep factual claims supported by the source or reviewed evidence. Illustrations are identified; no invented result, testimonial, number, map geometry or customer record.
3. **No recording is a supported mode.** Use `instructions/GRAPHICS.md` for authored animation. The generated silent base is only a rendering carrier; it is not filmed footage, a voiceover or a transcript. Review authored copy separately. Do not force a fake source or words into a source-free request.
4. **Measure real sources.** Run `node scripts/intake.mjs` for a new recording and use its `edit` path. Match cadence and HDR/SDR preparation to its measurements. Original media remains read-only.
5. **Review real moments.** In recordings use reviewed word starts plus transition boundaries; in graphics use planned action cues and resolved holds. Inspect actual previews and final output. Schema success, safe-zone success and sampled stills are not creative acceptance or uninterrupted playback.
6. **Natural presenter and legible sources.** Keep hair, face, beard, baked captions and meaningful screen details clear. Graphics effects act on graphics. Requested source grading may correct the image while preserving natural appearance; a new likeness treatment needs explicit permission.
7. **Scoped approval.** Propose uncertain cuts/privacy regions and new consequential creative choices before applying them. Preserve accepted plans, previews, source selections and explicit requests to finish. Continue authorized production without per-step reapproval; a request to produce the final still does not mean the creator accepted an unseen result. Ask before additional expensive work outside that scope.
8. **Live review and conflicts.** Open the live editor from the first prepared draft and keep it running. Save complete validated snapshots atomically. Reprepare changed cuts. Preserve manual drafts and resolve stale/pending saves before updating the same project.
9. **Sound and delivery.** Render through `node scripts/editor.mjs render`; keep the prepared sound stem. Delivery uses `--deliver` and all applicable source, plate, safe-zone, motion, face, audio and encode checks. Graphics-only work has no presenter/head plan; other gates remain. Fix the cause of a failed check.
10. **Profile and ending.** Preserve saved preferences and apply explicit choices to their requested scope. Use only the CTA, full handle, follow card and recorded sign-off applicable to this video, in their agreed order. Do not invent identity, a comment keyword or spoken ending, trim the recorded goodbye or cover it with a sting.
11. **Workspace and communication.** Outputs stay in `Projects/<slug>/` or `Outputs/<slug>/`; installs/scratch stay local. Normal edits do not change tracked engine files. Speak concise Egyptian Arabic unless asked otherwise; keep manifests/code/notes English UTF-8 without a BOM.
12. **Complete delivery.** Inspect the actual final MP4 and sound, report the scope of review, and include post descriptions written with the social-captions skill. Posting, publishing or changing accounts is a separate authorized action.
